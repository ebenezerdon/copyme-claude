# Usual lengths and shapes by platform

Use these when the user gives a platform but no length. Say which length you chose.

| Platform | Usual length | Shape |
| - | - | - |
| YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels | 30 to 45 s (75 to 110 words) | Hook in the first line, one idea, one example or proof, a one-line ending. No intro, no "in this video". |
| YouTube (long) | 6 to 10 min (900 to 1,500 words) | Hook (2 to 4 lines), what they'll get, sections with `##` labels, a turn or story in the middle, recap, one ask. |
| LinkedIn video | 45 to 90 s (110 to 225 words) | A plain first line that names the problem, a short story or lesson, the takeaway in one sentence. |
| Course lesson | 3 to 6 min (450 to 900 words) | What they'll be able to do, the steps in order with `##` labels, a quick recap. |
| Podcast intro or ad read | 30 to 60 s (75 to 150 words) | Who it's for, the one reason to keep listening, a clear handoff. |
| Sales or product demo | 60 to 90 s (150 to 225 words) | The pain, the moment it gets solved, proof, one call to action. |

A Short made from a longer script keeps its strongest single idea, not a summary of everything.
