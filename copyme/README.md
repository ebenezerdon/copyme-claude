# CopyMe for Claude

Write, polish and plan your talking-video scripts with Claude, and have them land in [CopyMe](https://copyme.cc), the teleprompter that follows your voice. Claude writes scripts to be spoken, at the length you need, in your own style, with B-roll notes and delivery marks where they help, then saves them straight to your CopyMe library so you can start reading on the prompter.

## What's inside

- **CopyMe connector** (`https://copyme.cc/mcp`): lets Claude list, read, create and edit your scripts, undo its own last edit, organize your library (status, tags, publish dates, platforms, archive) and save ideas. You sign in to CopyMe and choose Allow the first time.
- **Skills**: how to write a script for the prompter (length, hook, spoken style, markup), how to edit one without losing its formatting, and how to plan a content calendar.
- **Commands**: `/copyme:write`, `/copyme:polish`, `/copyme:plan`.

## Use it

Ask in plain words, for example:

- "Write a 45-second Short about why I film at 5 a.m. and save it to CopyMe."
- "Read my latest CopyMe script and tighten the hook."
- "Plan my next two weeks of videos in CopyMe, three a week."
- "Turn these notes into ten video ideas in CopyMe."

You need a CopyMe account. Saving and changing scripts needs a CopyMe plan; reading works on any account. Setup help: https://copyme.cc/claude

## Data

The plugin sends nothing on its own. The connector talks only to copyme.cc: Claude reads and writes the scripts you ask about, in your CopyMe account, over your signed-in connection. CopyMe keeps the previous version of a script before Claude's last change so it can be undone, and records which connector actions were used. Disconnect at any time in CopyMe under Settings, Account, Connected apps. Privacy policy: https://copyme.cc/privacy#claude
